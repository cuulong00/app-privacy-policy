Privacy Policy — QuanTriKenhKiemTien

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

QuanTriKenhKiemTien is an internal page publishing and analytics management utility operated strictly by the authorized administrators of its connected Facebook Pages.

1. Information We Access

Through the official Meta Graph API, this application accesses only the Facebook Page metadata, published posts, video/Reel insights, and public comments of Pages directly owned and authorized by the administrator. It does not collect, harvest, sell, or store personal data from external Facebook users.

2. How Information Is Used

Authorized Page data is used solely for scheduling original video content, reviewing aggregate Page performance metrics, and moderating Page comments on behalf of the Page owner.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not share, transfer, or sell any data to third parties. Access tokens are stored locally in encrypted operating-system keychains and can be revoked at any time via Facebook Business Integrations settings.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

This internal application does not store personal end-user profiles. If you wish to remove application access or request deletion of any associated records, you may:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations) and remove QuanTriKenhKiemTien; or
  2. Send a deletion request via direct message to the connected Facebook Page administration inbox, and all associated logs will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
